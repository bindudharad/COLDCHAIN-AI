Research Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

Formal framework of prediction-guided, energy-aware cold-chain optimization
Core Research Contribution
PREDICTION-GUIDED ENERGY-AWARE COLD-CHAIN OPTIMIZATION

Traditional refrigeration management operates reactively: cooling controllers only respond after temperatures breach upper bounds. COLDCHAIN AI uses a deep temporal convolutional network (MAD-TCN) to forecast future temperatures 15 steps ahead, calculates cumulative future risk, and feeds that risk into a multi-objective genetic algorithm (NSGA-II) to balance product safety and energy consumption.

Future Risk Formulation
Safety Objective

Given profile bounds $[T_{\min}, T_{\max}]$ and 15-step forecast $\mathbf{p} = [p_1, \dots, p_H]$:

V(p_i) = \max(p_i - T_{\max}, 0) + \max(T_{\min} - p_i, 0)
S_{\text{risk}} = \text{clip}\left(100 \cdot \left(\frac{\bar{V}}{T_{\max} - T_{\min} + \epsilon} + 0.08 \cdot \max(0, \overline{\Delta p})\right), 0, 100\right)

This penalty scales with both the average magnitude of boundary violation $\bar{V}$ and upward thermal velocity $\overline{\Delta p}$.

Estimated Energy Model
Energy Objective

Energy $E$ is simulated as a function of thermal lift $(T_{\text{ambient}} - T_{\text{chamber}})$, cooling intensity $I \in [0.1, 1.0]$, and compressor duty cycle $D \in [0.2, 1.0]$:

E = \left(0.25 + 0.035 \cdot \max(T_{\text{amb}} - T_{\text{curr}}, 0)\right) \cdot (0.35 + 0.65 \cdot I) \cdot D

NSGA-II minimizes residual risk and estimated energy simultaneously across a population of 48 individuals over 30 generations.
