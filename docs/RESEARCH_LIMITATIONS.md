# Scientific Honesty & Research Limitations

**Project:** COLDCHAIN-AI  
**Framework:** AI-Driven Big Data Analytics and Energy-Aware IoT for Cold Chain Logistics  
**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** OFFICIAL RESEARCH GOVERNANCE DISCLOSURE  

---

## 1. Physical Actuation Boundary
- **Status:** **NO PHYSICAL ACTUATION**
- **Details:** The recommendations generated by the prediction-guided NSGA-II optimizer (cooling intensity valve percentage, compressor duty cycle PWM, setpoint temperature offset) represent **software decision-support setpoints**.
- **Limitation:** The software has deliberately **not** been connected to physical electrical refrigeration contactors, high-voltage motor starters, or programmable logic controllers (PLCs). Connecting algorithmic setpoints to real industrial chillers requires redundant hardware safety interlocks, independent fail-safe thermal switches, and certification under ISO 22000 / HACCP food safety standards.

---

## 2. Electrical Energy Quantification
- **Status:** **ESTIMATED / SIMULATED**
- **Details:** All power (kW) and energy (kWh) figures reported across dashboards, APIs, and optimization frontiers are computed from empirical thermodynamic thermal-lift equations:
  $$E = \left(0.25 + 0.035 \cdot \max(T_{ambient} - T_{chamber}, 0)\right) \cdot (0.35 + 0.65 \cdot u) \cdot d$$
- **Limitation:** Power consumption is **not measured** using physical hardware power analyzers, Hall-effect current transformers, or utility revenue meters. While the mathematical model accurately reflects Carnot condensing lift dynamics, real compressor efficiency varies with refrigerant charge, superheat, fouling, and defrost cycles.

---

## 3. Dataset Scope & Domain Boundaries
- **Status:** **CALIBRATED SIMULATION + EXTERNAL BENCHMARKS**
- **Details:**
  - The baseline ground-truth telemetry (`simulated_cold_chain.csv`) was generated using a calibrated thermodynamic room differential model incorporating ambient infiltration ($k_{inf} = 0.018$), mechanical cooling ($k_{cool} = 0.32$), and stochastic door opening events.
  - Public datasets (`iot_temperature_readings.csv`, `energy_consumption_prediction.csv`) originate from residential rooms (ESP8266) and building HVAC facilities.
- **Limitation:** Public datasets are static historical downloads, not live streaming telemetry. Building HVAC energy models evaluate ambient air conditioning and are strictly separated from refrigeration metrics.

---

## 4. Big Data Scalability Scope
- **Status:** **SINGLE-NODE IN-MEMORY STREAMING (NOT DISTRIBUTED CLUSTER)**
- **Details:** The scalability benchmark evaluates high-performance single-node pandas/NumPy vectorization and PyTorch batching up to $1,000,000\text{ records}$ ($31.38\text{ seconds}$ total pipeline runtime, $251.8\text{ MB}$ peak RAM).
- **Limitation:** This demonstrates single-host streaming efficiency on edge or server nodes, but **does not constitute distributed enterprise Big Data infrastructure**. Distributed processing engines (such as Apache Spark, PySpark, Ray, or multi-node Kafka clusters) were not deployed.

---

## 5. Compute Hardware Environment
- **Status:** **EDGE / SERVER CPU EXECUTION**
- **Details:** MAD-TCN neural inference and NSGA-II evolutionary sorting execute purely on CPU hardware.
- **Limitation:** GPU acceleration (CUDA / TensorRT) is not required for 15-step univariate lookaheads at 1-minute intervals (batch latency is $< 75\text{ ms}$ on CPU).
