DOMAIN BOUNDARY DEFINITIONS

1. Cold-Chain Logistics Domain (SIMULATION):
Involves strict Food (0.0 to 5.0 °C) and Vaccine (2.0 to 8.0 °C) thresholds. Thermal inertia, compressor cycling, infiltration, and door events are physically modeled in simulated_cold_chain.csv.

2. Ambient Room IoT Domain (PUBLIC_DATASET):
ESP8266 telemetry captures room temperature variations (21 to 51 °C) in non-refrigerated living spaces. Used exclusively to validate temporal convolutional sequence learning on real hardware noise.

3. Building Energy Demand Domain (PUBLIC_DATASET):
Captures residential HVAC electricity demand based on occupancy and ambient temperature. Validates empirical thermal-lift equations, but is never confused with refrigeration compressor wattage.
